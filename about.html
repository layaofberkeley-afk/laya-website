About Laya

Keeping Carnatic music alive at Berkeley

A community of students playing, singing and sharing Indian classical music since 2015

What is Carnatic Music?

Carnatic music is the classical music of South India, with roots going back centuries. It's built on ragas (melodic frameworks) and talas (rhythmic cycles), and much of it is improvised live, so no two performances sound the same.

Story

Our Story

Laya (n.): speed or tempo, the union of sound

Laya of Berkeley is a unique music club at the University of California, Berkeley. Founded in 2015, we aim to bring the intricacies and flows of Carnatic and Hindustani classical music to the diverse, cosmopolitan environment of Cal and the larger Bay Area. Comprised of vocalists and instrumentalists, we perform pure classical pieces as well as fusions with other classical and Western styles. We also invite and host professionals and performers in lectures and concerts. In all our endeavors we hope to find and foster the rhythm of Berkeley and bring different parts of the world together through our art.
